123 App - Account Deletion

Users of the 123 App can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to Request Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your account directly from the Settings section of the 123 App using the Delete Account option.

If you are unable to access the application, you may also contact us using the contact information provided below and request deletion of your account.

Data That Will Be Deleted

When an account deletion request is processed, personal information associated with the account will be deleted where applicable, including:

  • Name and profile information
  • Phone number associated with the account
  • Address and other user-provided information
  • Business information associated with the account
  • User-uploaded images and documents associated with the account

Data Retention

Some information may need to be retained where required by law, regulatory requirements, security purposes, or legitimate business requirements. Any retained information will be kept only for the period necessary for those purposes.

Processing Time

Account deletion requests will be processed within 30 days.
